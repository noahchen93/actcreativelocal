Choose the operating model

Two practical ways to put a mobile unit on site

Choose based on lead time, customization and ownership needs.

Faster route

Rent and customize a Singapore sample unit

Use an existing mobile unit as the base, then adapt its exterior, signage, internal setup and event-facing details for a campaign or activation.

  • Suitable for short-term events, pop-ups and market testing
  • Lower initial commitment than a full custom build
  • Brand wrap, signs, serving setup and selected equipment integration
  • Local delivery, setup and handover can be scoped separately
Purpose-built route

Design, build and purchase a custom unit

Develop the unit around a specific operating model, visual identity, equipment list and rollout plan, with China production and Singapore-side coordination.

  • Suitable for repeated use, roadshows, mobile retail and owned assets
  • Greater control over dimensions, structure, finish and internal layout
  • Production follow-up, export packing and freight planning
  • Local delivery, installation, commissioning and support options
Plan approvals early. Operating permissions and food-related approvals depend on the final configuration, operator, equipment, site and current authority requirements. A unit is not automatically approved for every use.

FRP and sculptural fabrication

FRP sculpture fabrication for Singapore events, exhibitions and brand installations

ACT Creative supports FRP sculpture, sculptural props and lightweight installation fabrication for projects that require custom forms, durable finishes and controlled production coordination.

Where FRP works well

Large sculptural props

Hero objects, characters, product replicas and display features that need volume without excessive weight.

Outdoor activations

Event and retail pieces requiring stronger surfaces, controlled finishing and transport-ready construction.

Exhibition displays

Special forms, display shells, themed elements and brand environments that standard carpentry cannot deliver.

Production replicas

Repeated sculptural parts, moulded shapes and consistent finishes for campaign rollouts.

Production planning

FRP projects need careful planning around sculpting, moulding, structure, finishing, curing time, packing and site handling. ACT Creative can coordinate the production brief, sample references, surface finish, reinforcement requirements and shipment plan before fabrication starts.

For Singapore and regional projects, China-side production can be practical when the schedule allows for sampling, quality checks and shipping. ACT Creative helps bridge that process for event agencies and brands that need bilingual coordination.

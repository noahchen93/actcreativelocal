Interactive digital display FAQ

What systems can you provide?

Depending on the brief, we can coordinate interactive rail screens, touchscreens, kiosks, tables, holographic displays, projection, LED and video walls, content publishing and central control.

Do you manufacture the hardware?

ACT Creative manages the Singapore-facing brief and delivery. Specialist hardware manufacturing and technical integration are coordinated with vetted partners according to the approved scope.

Can you install and commission in Singapore?

Yes. A proposal can include receiving, installation coordination, content and system checks, commissioning, client acceptance and an agreed aftercare route.

Can you also make the interactive content?

Content can be included or coordinated when the format, languages, interaction, source assets and approval process are defined early. Hardware and content responsibilities are listed separately.

How early should we start?

Start as early as possible. Custom hardware, content production, testing, freight and venue preparation can run on different timelines. We confirm a programme after the site and technical assumptions are clear.

Can the system be rented?

Some standard components may have rental routes, while custom cabinets, rail systems and permanent installations are usually project-built. We will compare suitable options against duration and budget.

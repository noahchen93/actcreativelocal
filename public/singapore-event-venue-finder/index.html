Singapore venue comparison guide

How to compare event venues in Singapore

Use the map as a first-pass planning tool, then verify the operational details that determine whether a venue can support the event.

Start with location and guest movement

Marina Bay and the Civic District suit central business events, Orchard and Tanglin offer established hotel options, while Sentosa and waterfront areas support destination-style programmes.

Compare the venue type and setting

Filter hotels, restaurants, convention venues, galleries and outdoor spaces. Then compare indoor, outdoor and waterfront settings against access, weather cover and production requirements.

Treat capacity as a planning signal

Published capacity depends on room layout, staging, catering and safety requirements. Shortlist suitable options before requesting a site-specific review.

From creative reference to production detail

Custom prop production needs more than a visual reference. ACT Creative helps define dimensions, materials, structure, finishing, lighting requirements, transport constraints and site assembly details. This makes the quotation clearer and reduces production surprises later in the timeline.

For complex or cost-sensitive props, the team can coordinate China suppliers for fabrication while keeping Singapore-side communication practical for agencies and event producers.

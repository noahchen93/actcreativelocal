Material route

Choose a practical build method for the prop

The right material is selected after considering scale, surface finish, weight, audience contact, transport, site conditions and whether the asset must be reused.

Lightweight sculpted forms

Useful for large visual volumes where manageable weight and efficient transport matter.

FRP or fiberglass

Suitable for durable shaped objects, character forms and finished display pieces that need a rigid shell.

Wood, metal or mixed builds

Useful when a prop needs a structural frame, flat display surfaces, hardware or integrated functional details.

Fabric or inflatable structures

Potential routes for soft forms, compact packing or large-volume temporary installations, subject to site conditions.

Custom props FAQ

What materials can be used for custom event props?

The production route depends on size, finish, audience contact, transport and reuse. Options can include lightweight sculpted forms, FRP or fiberglass, wood, metal, mixed materials, fabric and inflatable structures.

What information is needed for a custom prop quotation?

Send reference images, dimensions, quantity, indoor or outdoor use, venue, event date, finish expectations, audience interaction, installation requirements and whether the prop will be reused.

Can production be coordinated in China for a Singapore event?

Yes. When the timeline and project route are suitable, ACT Creative can coordinate China-side supplier communication, production checks, packing and delivery planning for Singapore use.

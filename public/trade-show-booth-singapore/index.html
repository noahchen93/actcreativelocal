Singapore venue experience

Each Singapore venue has its own rigging rules, build-up timelines, drayage requirements and on-site contractor procedures. ACT Creative supports exhibitors at Marina Bay Sands Expo & Convention Centre, Singapore EXPO, Suntec Singapore Convention Centre, Sands Expo and partner industry venues, working within official organiser guidelines for stand approval, electrical risers and dismantle windows.

For overseas exhibitors entering Singapore, the team can localise booth designs from a master kit, fabricate panels and structural elements in China, ship into Singapore, and run on-site assembly and dismantle.

What we deliver

  • Booth concept and 3D rendering aligned to exhibitor brand standards
  • Engineered drawings for venue submission and approval
  • Fabrication of structures, panels, signage, counters, plinths and lightboxes
  • On-site assembly, A/V integration, lighting, dressing and dismantle
  • Custom merchandise and giveaways for booth visitors

Why Greater Bay Area beats long-haul

Hong Kong sits next door to one of the densest manufacturing regions in Asia. ACT Creative's supply chain spans Shenzhen, Dongguan, Guangzhou and Foshan — that means same-day factory visits for QC, shorter shipping lead times and fewer customs handoffs compared with shipping props or fabrication from Singapore or other markets.

For agencies running campaigns across both HK and SEA, ACT Creative consolidates production once in China and delivers in parallel to HK and Singapore. Same supplier network, same project manager, two destinations.

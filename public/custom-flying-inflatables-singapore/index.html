Frequently asked

Practical questions before production starts

Can flying inflatables be used outdoors?

Controlled flying systems are primarily recommended for indoor venues. Outdoor concepts require a separate assessment of wind, weather, operating boundaries, aviation considerations and contingency procedures.

Can you create a custom mascot or logo?

Yes. The shape, colour, branding, lighting and movement can be developed from reference artwork, subject to feasibility and venue constraints.

Do we need a technical operator?

For first-time teams and complex shows, professional installation, rehearsal and operating support is strongly recommended. The level of support depends on the system and show plan.

What should we send for a quotation?

Send the venue, clear height, reference design, target size, event date, operating time, delivery location and whether installation or technical support is needed.

Planning a flying centerpiece for your next event?

Send the venue plan, reference image, target date and preferred size. We will help evaluate the concept and turn it into a workable production brief.

Why agencies use China production support

China production can reduce cost and expand booth fabrication options, but it also adds risk if specifications, language, sampling and packing are not controlled. ACT Creative works as the bridge between Singapore project needs and China-side suppliers, helping teams avoid vague quotations and late production surprises.

This is useful for brand activations, exhibitions, mall campaigns, roadshows, launch events and multi-market campaign assets.

Common deliverables we coordinate

Typical production lists include oversized props, foam and FRP sculptures, acrylic display units, stage scenic pieces, product launch plinths, exhibition booth components, signage, LED-ready structures, event merchandise, press kits and branded packing. Each item is checked against the brief for dimensions, material, finish, durability, packing method and destination constraints before a factory quote is treated as usable.

For Singapore event agencies, this creates a single accountable workflow: the agency keeps the client relationship and creative direction, while ACT Creative handles China supplier communication, production photos, sample review, QC notes and shipment readiness. For campaigns that also need Hong Kong or Macau delivery, the same China-side supply chain can support event fabrication in Hong Kong without rebuilding the supplier network from zero.

When to involve ACT Creative

  • Before quoting, when you need a realistic China production cost for custom props, booth pieces or campaign merchandise.
  • Before artwork approval, when fabrication drawings, dimensions, finishes and packing details still need to be made production-ready.
  • During production, when the client needs photo updates, sample feedback, defect checks and clearer deadline control.
  • Before shipping, when boxes, crates, labels and consolidation need to match Singapore or regional event handover requirements.

About ACT Creative

An integrated solutions partner for ideas that need to happen

ACT Creative is a Singapore-based service and sourcing partner for exhibitions, offline events and other custom projects that need to move from an idea into a practical result. We assemble the right mix of specialist suppliers, production routes, logistics and local execution around each brief.

Looking for a specific service? Start with our dedicated event fabrication services in Singapore, exhibition booth design and build contractor page or international supplier sourcing support.

What our role is

We are not tied to one factory, contractor or fixed catalogue. Our role is to understand the outcome, compare suitable suppliers and sourcing options, identify the risks, and assemble one accountable solution. Clients work with one coordinating team while we manage the specialist partners required behind the scenes.

Our event and project experience helps us compare more than price. We consider material suitability, production capability, quality control, lead time, packing, freight, site conditions, local regulations and installation requirements before recommending a route.

Why clients use ACT Creative

What one-stop support can include

Supplier sourcing and comparison

Identify and compare specialist manufacturers, contractors, venues, installers and service providers suited to the project.

Cross-border production and shipping

Coordinate production in the most suitable market, then plan packing, consolidation, air or sea freight and delivery timing.

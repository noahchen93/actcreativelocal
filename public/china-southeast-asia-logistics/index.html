China-SEA Event Logistics Coordination

Event assets are not normal parcels. Booth components, props, displays, merchandise and artworks need packing, labeling, consolidation and delivery timing that match venue access and event setup windows. ACT Creative coordinates the production-side and delivery-side details so teams can see what is moving, when it is moving and what needs attention before arrival.

What we coordinate

Packing requirements

Boxing, crating, foam protection, fragile labels, item numbering and photo records before shipment.

Consolidation

Combining multiple suppliers or item categories into a clearer shipment plan where practical.

Timeline planning

Back-planning production completion, pickup, transit, delivery and buffer time against event setup windows.

Handover coordination

Delivery contact, loading bay notes, installation team coordination and missing-item checks at arrival.

How we provide the service

  • List what needs to move, from which city, to which venue, by what date.
  • Check whether items are finished, packed, labeled and photo-documented before pickup.
  • Coordinate with logistics partners or client-appointed forwarders around pickup, volume and destination requirements.
  • Flag oversize items, fragile builds, special venue access, customs-sensitive categories and urgent timelines.

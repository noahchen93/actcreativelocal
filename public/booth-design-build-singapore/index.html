Booth design and build FAQ

Can ACT Creative work from a reference image instead of final booth drawings?

Yes. ACT Creative can begin with references, floor plans and brand requirements, then help turn the idea into a buildable scope for quotation and production.

Which Singapore venues can you support for exhibition booth setup?

ACT Creative can support booth planning and setup coordination for Marina Bay Sands Expo & Convention Centre, Singapore EXPO, Suntec Singapore Convention Centre, Sands Expo, Resorts World Sentosa and other Singapore exhibition venues, subject to organiser rules and access windows.

What information is needed for a booth design and build quote?

Useful quote inputs include booth size, floor plan, venue, event dates, reference images, brand guidelines, required counters or displays, storage needs, lighting or AV requirements, setup and teardown timing, and a target budget range.

Do you only build in Singapore?

Singapore is the main delivery market, but ACT Creative can coordinate China-side production for booth components and regional delivery when the timeline allows.

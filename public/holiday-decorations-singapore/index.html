Turnkey process from loose idea to reinstated site

Festive decoration projects are short-window, high-visibility installations. ACT Creative keeps the work practical by treating design, sourcing, production, compliance coordination, maintenance and dismantling as one connected delivery plan.

01

Brief

Collect venue, dates, audience, campaign objective, budget range, site photos, references and operational constraints.

02

Concept route

Translate the goal into a buildable seasonal direction: tree, lantern, sculpture, lighting, photo spot, stage or full-space dressing.

03

Budget route

Compare rental, standard components, local fabrication, overseas custom production and reusable modular options.

04

Sourcing

Coordinate suitable materials, props, lights, structures, ornaments and fabrication suppliers in Singapore or China.

05

Fabrication

Manage production checks for scale, finish, packing, transport, installation method and site-ready delivery.

06

Site coordination

Plan access, manpower, power, fixing, protection, qualified-party coordination and venue submission support where required.

07

Maintenance

Support display-period checks, light replacement, cleaning routes and emergency response planning when the installation runs for weeks.

08

Dismantling

Coordinate teardown, site reinstatement, packing, disposal, storage or reuse planning after the festive campaign ends.

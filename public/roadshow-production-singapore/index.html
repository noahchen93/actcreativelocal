Multi-venue brand campaigns

Roadshow production for Singapore brand campaigns and Southeast Asia tours

ACT Creative builds modular, repeatable roadshow sets that travel — designed once in Singapore, fabricated in China, deployed across malls, retail venues and SEA cities without losing brand consistency.

What roadshow production covers

Modular set design

Flat-pack walls, branded archways, demo counters, photo zones and lockable storage that crews can assemble in 4-6 hours.

Repeatable signage

Replaceable graphic panels, swappable date/venue plates and multi-language overlays for SEA tour stops.

Tour logistics

Reusable transport cases, packing manifests, cross-border customs paperwork and venue-by-venue load-in scheduling.

On-site crews

Assembly, dressing, brand ambassador uniforms, dismantle and storage-between-stops support.

Built to survive multiple deployments

Roadshow sets fail when production treats them as one-off event builds. ACT Creative designs every element with repeat assembly cycles, durable surfaces, replaceable graphics and weight-aware transport in mind. That means lighter aluminium frames where possible, scratch-resistant panel finishes, modular cable management and clear assembly diagrams crew teams can follow without the original installer present.

For SEA tours starting in Singapore, the team can also localise graphics, swap merchandise SKUs by market, and route the kit through China for reset and refresh between legs.

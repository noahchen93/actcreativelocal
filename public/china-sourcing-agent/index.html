Sea, air or combined?

Large and non-urgent goods usually favour sea freight. Urgent or high-priority items may need air freight. Mixed product lines often need a combined plan.

For customized event merchandise, start 6-8 weeks ahead when possible. Earlier planning gives better supplier options and lower emergency cost.

Sea FreightBest for large volume, non-urgent goods.Risk: longer lead time.
Air FreightBest for urgent or high-priority items.Risk: higher cost.
Combined PlanBest for mixed product lines and staggered supplier timelines.Risk: requires coordination.

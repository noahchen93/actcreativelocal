K11 Shenyang — 30+ Large-Scale Public Artworks Collection

A multi-piece public art collection delivered at K11 Shenyang. ACT Creative coordinated production, finishing and precision installation across 30+ large-scale works inside a live mall environment with exacting artistic standards.

Project facts

Venue

K11 Shenyang, China

Scope

30+ large-scale public artworks across the venue.

Standard

Precise installation meeting exacting artistic standards.

Environment

Live retail / mall space with footfall and operating hours constraints.

Why mall-scale public art is harder than gallery installation

Installing 30+ public artworks inside an operating shopping mall is a different problem from a gallery: load-in windows are short, structural anchoring has to satisfy mall engineering, footfall protection and hoarding plans have to be approved, and final finishing has to be venue-clean rather than studio-clean.

ACT Creative managed the work from production-end (fabrication coordination across the China supply chain network including Foshan and Guangzhou for FRP and structural elements) through to install-end (rigging, anchoring, lighting calibration and final colour-matching against artist reference samples).

Why mall and retail venues choose ACT Creative

  • Multi-piece coordination — keeping 30+ works on parallel production timelines without compromising any single piece
  • Venue-aware install — aligning to mall operating hours, structural rules and hoarding plans
  • Precision finishing — colour-matching and surface integrity that reads correctly in mall LED lighting, not studio light
  • Cross-border logistics — China factory hubs into the venue with QC and packing accountability all the way to the loading dock

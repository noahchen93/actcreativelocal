Florentijn Hofman — First Solo Touring Exhibition in China

A first-of-its-kind touring solo exhibition in China for Dutch artist Florentijn Hofman, with ACT Creative handling end-to-end production: fabrication coordination, installation engineering and on-site delivery across museum venues.

High visitor engagement and met revenue targets.

Why this matters for cross-border art production

Touring international artists into Chinese museum venues requires more than transport — it needs structural drawings translated for local engineers, fabrication of replacement and supplementary pieces near the venue, and on-site installation crews that can interpret the artist's intent without losing fidelity.

ACT Creative's role on this project covered fabrication coordination across China supply chain hubs, FRP and structural element production, packing and crating logic, multi-venue install scheduling and quality control across the tour.

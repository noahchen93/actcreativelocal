Craig & Karl — First Solo Touring Exhibition in China

A first-of-its-kind touring solo exhibition in China for design duo Craig & Karl. ACT Creative handled the full execution chain — from initial concept translation through fabrication, packing and museum-grade installation across the tour.

Translating bold graphic design into physical exhibition

Craig & Karl's signature is high-saturation pattern and bold graphic language. Translating that into a museum-scale installation needs careful work on colour matching, surface finish, lighting calibration and typography fabrication. Slight drift on colour or finish breaks the artist's signal.

ACT Creative coordinated colour-matching to the artists' Pantone references on each fabricated surface, ran finish samples through approval before full production, and managed packing and transport between Beijing and tour venues without quality drift.

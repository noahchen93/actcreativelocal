Singapore · Event merchandise & materials

ARTBOX Singapore event merchandise sourcing case

ACT Creative provided procurement, custom fabrication and logistics coordination for ARTBOX Singapore. The scope covered branded merchandise, retail items, event materials and supporting logistics — delivered as a single coordinated workflow.

Scope of work

Procurement

Supplier matching, quotation comparison, sample requests and approval rounds for retail and giveaway items used at the event.

Customization

Custom fabrication and printing on merchandise, packaging and supporting materials — finishes and colours coordinated to the event brief.

Logistics

Packing, palletising, freight coordination and Singapore-side handover so items arrived ready for distribution at the venue.

Quality coordination

Pre-shipment quality checks and replacement coordination when needed, reducing on-the-ground surprises during the event run.

Why this matters

Events with strong retail and merchandise components live or die on consistency. A single supplier slip — wrong colour, late delivery, inconsistent finish across a batch — is visible the moment guests arrive. ACT Creative absorbed that coordination load: one Singapore point of contact, multiple production routes, with quality checks before items moved.

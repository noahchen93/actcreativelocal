The client's brief, simplified

The client gives requirements and receives finished goods

The operating idea was straightforward: the client should not have to separately manage factory discovery, production communication, international shipping, schedule pressure and last-mile delivery. ACT Creative held those moving parts together and provided one local point of accountability.

Frequently asked

Custom mug sourcing for events in Singapore

Can ACT Creative source custom mugs other than this enamel style?

Yes. The sourcing process can begin from a reference image, intended use, quantity, branding method, budget range and required delivery date. Factory options are then matched to the actual brief.

Can one order include multiple artwork versions?

It can, subject to the factory's minimum quantity and production setup. Multi-version orders need clear artwork naming, quantity allocation and packing identification before production.

Can you manage delivery all the way to a Singapore venue or office?

Yes. International freight and local transport can be planned as part of the same delivery workflow, with the final handover point agreed in the brief.

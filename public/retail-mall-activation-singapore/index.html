ACT Creative produces mall pop-ups, atrium installations, themed photo moments and seasonal fabrication for Singapore retail venues — designed locally, manufactured in China, installed in your venue's load-in window.

Activation formats we deliver

Branded retail kiosks, merchandise zones, demo counters and themed walls for ION, VivoCity, Jewel and other anchor mall atriums.

Themed photo moments

FRP sculpture characters, oversized props, immersive backdrops and selfie installations engineered for footfall and durability.

Brand collaborations

IP and licensing-led activations: anime, gaming, lifestyle and entertainment crossover builds with character props and merch.

Built around mall operating rules

Each Singapore mall has its own atrium load-bearing rules, lift-access dimensions, overnight install windows, fire-safety material requirements and design submission timelines. ACT Creative builds against these constraints from concept stage — sketches, structural calculations, material certificates and venue paperwork all aligned before fabrication starts. That's the difference between a clean install and a 2am scramble at the loading dock.

For multi-venue campaigns, the same kit can travel between malls — see also roadshow production for tour-ready set design.

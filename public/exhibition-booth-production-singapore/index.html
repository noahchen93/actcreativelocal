Booth fabrication support

Booth fabrication and exhibition booth production support in Singapore

ACT Creative supports exhibition contractors, event agencies and brand teams with custom booth components, display props, counters, signage, lightboxes and production sourcing for Singapore and regional exhibitions.

This is a production-focused support page for booth components and custom exhibition assets. For the main service workflow covering brief intake, design review, quotation inputs, production planning and installation coordination, see Booth Design & Build in Singapore.

What this booth fabrication page is for

Use this page when you already have a booth concept, approved design direction, component list or production drawing, and need help turning those items into physical booth parts. This is different from a full booth design and build page: the focus here is fabrication, supplier coordination, display component production, packing and readiness for Singapore installation.

Production scope

Booth components

Counters, plinths, display stands, product shelves, arches, signage structures and modular feature pieces.

Brand display props

Product replicas, themed decor, photo features and visual merchandising assets for exhibition environments.

Lightboxes and signage

Backlit logos, fascia elements, graphic panels, wayfinding signs and branded structures for exhibition booth environments.

China sourcing

Factory coordination for custom parts, merchandise, lightweight structures, acrylic, metal, wood and printed components.

Logistics planning

Packing, labeling, shipment timing and handover details for smooth local installation planning.

Useful for time-sensitive exhibition projects

Exhibition work usually requires fast decisions and practical coordination across design, production, transport and installation. ACT Creative helps translate booth concepts into production-ready requirements, including size, finish, assembly, packing and deadline constraints.

The team can support one-off custom pieces or recurring assets for roadshows and regional campaign rollouts.

When to use this production support page

  • You already have a booth concept, drawing or component list and need fabrication support.
  • You need custom counters, product display props, lightboxes, signage structures or modular booth parts.
  • You want China-side production coordination for booth components before Singapore delivery.
  • You are an agency or contractor that needs production help, not a full creative booth design package.

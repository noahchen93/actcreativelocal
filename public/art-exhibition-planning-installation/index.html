What this service covers

Planning support

Translate curatorial direction, artist intent, venue constraints and visitor flow into a practical display plan.

Fabrication coordination

Coordinate plinths, walls, scenic elements, FRP pieces, signage, display structures and protective details.

Installation planning

Plan site sequence, manpower, access, tools, touch-up work, safety notes and final handover checkpoints.

Touring and regional support

Support repeat installations, China-side production and Southeast Asia delivery when exhibitions travel.

How we provide the service

  • Review floor plans, artwork list, dimensions, photos, audience flow and venue restrictions.
  • Clarify plinth height, wall structure, object support, mounting, lighting-ready details and protection needs.
  • Coordinate fabrication drawings, material samples, colour references, production photos and packing.
  • Organize delivery timing, site access, installation sequence, adjustments and handover documentation.

## Public-use safeguards

- Public labels use “manufacturing network reference” and generic production-route descriptions.
- No supplier name, logo or private partner contact detail is published.
- The website describes ACT Creative's role as scope definition, route selection, production coordination, quality follow-up, logistics and site-delivery integration.
- Any claim of exclusivity, authorisation, certification, capacity, price or lead time requires project-specific verification.

## Purpose

This record documents the partner materials used to create ACT Creative's interactive digital display service pages:

- English: `/interactive-digital-display-singapore/`
- Chinese: `/zh/interactive-digital-display-singapore/`

The page positions ACT Creative as the Singapore-facing project and delivery coordinator. It does not claim that ACT Creative manufactures the hardware or holds an exclusive distributorship.

## Supplied source material

The user-supplied partner folder is local and is not published. It contains:

- one technology presentation

The presentation describes specialist display-manufacturing capabilities including custom production, OEM/ODM and display-system integration. Product references include interactive rail screens, touch displays, LED and video walls, holographic displays, projection, information publishing and exhibition control systems.

## Public-use wording

The service pages use these safeguards:

- Media is labelled as partner-supplied technology reference material, not as an ACT Creative client case study.
- No supplier name or logo is shown; ACT Creative is presented as the client-facing systems and solutions integration platform.
- The page does not describe the relationship as exclusive, authorised or official.
- Client enquiries and public contact details route through ACT Creative.
- Private partner contact details from the supplied presentation are not published.

Before changing the relationship wording to “official agent”, “authorised distributor” or “exclusive representative”, obtain written confirmation covering territory, brand usage, quotation authority, warranty, after-sales responsibility and lead ownership.
